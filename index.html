How we treat each other

  • Be kind.Critique the work, never the person.
  • Be specific.Say what you need, or what you’d improve.
  • Give before you take.Help someone each time you ask for help.
  • No spam.Share your work in Show & Review, not as random promotions.
  • Respect privacy.Don’t forward or screenshot people’s work without permission.

Questions

Who can join?

Developers, designers, creators, business owners and students. If you’re building something, or want to learn how, you belong here. Beginners are welcome.

What happens when I tap Join?

The link opens the Minovates WhatsApp group. An admin may approve your request first. Once you’re in, introduce yourself with your name, what you do, and what you’re building or learning.

How active is it?

There’s a simple weekly rhythm: a Monday goals thread, a Wednesday Skill Drop and Friday Wins. Help requests and work reviews happen any time, so you can join in as much or as little as you like.

Do I need to be experienced?

No. You’ll learn from people a few steps ahead, and help people a few steps behind. Everyone has something to give.

Who runs Minovates?
